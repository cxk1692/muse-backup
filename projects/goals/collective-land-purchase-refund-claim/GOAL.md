# Collective land purchase refund claim

Goal ID: goal_39ce1c6ae0c5
Goal slug: collective-land-purchase-refund-claim

## Description
Kuai's late father Zheng Xuqiang bought two plots of collective land in 2020 for ¥243,000 in cash, but building was stopped in 2021 and the house was demolished because the land is red-line cultivated land. The land remains unbuildable, so the refund claim now passes to his heirs, with negotiation or mediation first and then a lawsuit to confirm the contract invalid and recover the payment.
