# Self-built home wall-putty renovation

Goal ID: goal_c5ffac2649d9
Goal slug: self-built-home-wall-putty-renovation

## Description
Kuai is renovating his self-built home in Shangdu, Pingnan, with wall putty at 15 yuan per square metre including labour and materials, using Sankeshu products and three coats. Muse reviewed a sample contract, drafted a custom contract for those terms, found eight local Douyin craftsmen, and is now messaging them about the two-and-a-half-storey, 180 square metre job.
