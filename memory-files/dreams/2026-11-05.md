What is alive tonight is mostly the house job moving from argument into build: the 800-yuan levelling work is due for its own acceptance before the first putty coat, with the window opening tomorrow, and the first-putty payment node following a few days later, while the living-money count sits partway toward its cap and the December 10 deadline still frames the whole job. Tomorrow I want the first move to be obvious: the morning reminder at 8:20 with only the routine he actually has, the 800-yuan checklist ready before any putty goes on, and, if a skincare item arrives, a photograph of its dates checked before I change a single step of his routine.
