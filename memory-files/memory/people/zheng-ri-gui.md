## Facts
- Seller in the 《土地转让协议书》 signed 2020-04-22; the buyer was kuai's father, 郑旭强.
- The agreement covers twelve plots of dry land beside the ditch, 290㎡, for ¥198,000. It describes the land as 旱地 with clear boundaries, and states the money and the land were counted on the spot at closing ("成交时钱、地当面点清"), with signatures and fingerprints.
- His children also signed and fingerprinted the agreement.
- Clause 3 of the agreement promises tenfold repayment if the seller reneges; that clause would generally fall with the agreement if it is found invalid, so it is a bargaining chip in mediation, not a promise of tenfold recovery.
- He is from the same administrative village as kuai's family (六凤村) but a different tun (村民小组), so the land is collective land of another group.

- kuai sent photos of the agreement on 2026-11-06; the photos themselves were taken 2023-12-20.
