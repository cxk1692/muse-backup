# 郑旭强

## Facts
- kuai's father; kuai calls him "我爸" / "爸".
- In 2020 he bought collective land from fellow villagers of 六凤村 — the same administrative village as kuai's family, but a different tun (村民小组) — paying cash.
- Two agreements, both signed 2020-04-22; kuai sent photos of them on 2026-11-06 (the photos themselves were taken 2023-12-20):
  - 《土地转让协议书》: seller 郑日桂; twelve plots of dry land beside the ditch, 290㎡, ¥198,000; the agreement describes the land as 旱地 with clear boundaries; clause 3 promises tenfold repayment if the seller reneges, but that clause would generally fall with the agreement if it is found invalid, so it is not a promise of tenfold recovery; the seller's children also signed and fingerprinted; a witness signed one copy, handwriting pending kuai's confirmation.
  - 《土地转让证据》: sellers 郑荣华, 郑荣桂, 郑荣新 — names read from the photos, pending kuai's confirmation; a strip in front of the house-building plot, 13.5 m by 3.8 m (about 51.3㎡), ¥45,000, for long-term use in front of the gate ("门前长期使用").
- Total paid: ¥243,000. Both agreements state the money and the land were counted on the spot at closing ("成交时钱、地当面点清"), with signatures and fingerprints — this largely answers the cash-payment evidence problem.
- kuai described the purchase in conversation as "200多平" for "20多万"; the agreement figures above are the documented ones.
- There was one witness to the transaction, who was not a village-committee member at the time and now is a village-committee staff member handling land management.
- The land is red-line cultivated land (kuai confirmed the land class himself, 2026-11-06); building on it is not possible.
- In 2021 he started building on the plot; work was stopped and a rectification notice issued; he demolished the building himself. The notice, issued by the Shangdu Town Comprehensive Law Enforcement Bureau, states only "占用集体土地建房、未经审批" and does not name the land class.
- The plot now lies idle and unused.
- He has died. kuai told the assistant on 2026-11-06: "我爸已经走了". The date of death was not asked and is not known.

## History
- 2020-04-22: signed both land agreements and paid ¥243,000 in cash, money and land counted on the spot.
- 2021: started building on the plot; stopped by a rectification notice; demolished the building himself.
- 2021–2026: the plot stayed unbuildable and the purchase money stayed sunk; the family found it distressing.
- 2026-11-06: kuai told the assistant his father had died. The refund claim for the purchase money passes to the heirs as inheritance; the route stays the same — negotiate or mediate a refund, then sue to confirm the agreements invalid and recover the money if needed — with the suing party now the heirs rather than him. kuai was told not to rush it that evening.
