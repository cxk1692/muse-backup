# Verify Figures Before Totals

## Lock the inputs before you calculate

- Restate the scope in plain words before you calculate any total.

- Confirm the unit for every quantity and convert it before you multiply.

- List what the total includes and what it excludes before you present it.

- Ask the user to confirm an input when the scope, unit or inclusion is ambiguous.

- Hold the calculation until every ambiguous input has an answer or is marked as an assumption.

## Label the source of every figure

- Label each figure as a receipt, a measured quantity, a contractor claim, a posted rate or an estimate.

- Keep a posted rate, an estimate and a paid receipt in separate lines and never merge them into one figure.

- Mark a contractor claim or an unverified sum as unverified and keep it out of a settled total.

- Ask for the source document or measurement when a figure arrives without one.

- Present an unverified figure only as a question to check and never as a settled amount.

## Book a payment only on a confirmed amount

- Ask the user for the amount and the purpose before you record any payment as paid.

- Treat a phrase such as transferred to him without an amount as unconfirmed and do not book a round figure from it.

- Mark an unconfirmed payment as tentative and keep it out of a settled total until the amount and purpose are confirmed.

- Use the written record over a later recollection when the two disagree and name the record you used.

- Recheck the rebuilt ledger after a correction and remove any earlier total that still uses the corrected payment.

## Show the arithmetic and what changed

- Show the full arithmetic for every total so the user can check each step.

- Show the previous total beside a revised total and name the input that changed.

- Recalculate from the locked inputs after a correction instead of patching only the final number.

- Check the rebuilt total for any remaining input that still uses the corrected value incorrectly.

- Deliver a total only after the scope, unit, source and arithmetic all agree.

## Alternatives for better results

- Offer a range with its assumptions stated when the inputs cannot be locked yet.

- Split a mixed total into separate totals when different parts carry different sources.

- Ask the user to choose between two readings when two scopes remain plausible instead of choosing one silently.
