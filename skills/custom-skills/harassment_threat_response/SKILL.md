# Harassment Threat Response

## Close evidence collection once it is enough

- List the evidence already held and the route it is meant to support before you ask for more.

- Declare collection closed in plain words once the held evidence is enough for that route.

- Refuse further unblocking, answering or listening for evidence after you declare collection closed, even when the user asks to collect more.

- Compile a timeline or statement only from evidence already held after collection closes.

- Reopen collection only when a new route needs a specific missing item and name that item first.

## Block first and protect sleep

- Tell the user to block the number and account and to stop answering calls and messages once collection closes.

- Keep the user reachable only through a safe channel and do not ask the user to stay reachable for evidence.

- Put sleep and safety ahead of any further message and say so when the user is awake late because of the threats.

- Offer a short written reply the user can send once and then ignore further contact, instead of a reply that invites another round.

## Do not trade a promise you cannot deliver

- State up front when a requested action is not yours to deliver, such as unblocking an account that only its owner can appeal.

- Give the correct appeal route once and do not promise a result from it.

- Refuse a concession offered only to buy a brief calm when the other party has resumed threats after earlier calm.

- Move any concession to a mediated setting and say that setting in the reply instead of promising it in a direct message.

## Describe police and legal steps without overpromising

- Distinguish a warning, an administrative registration, a detention decision and a criminal matter as separate outcomes.

- State that a warning followed by a repeat does not reliably lead to detention or a crime and name the uncertainty.

- Ask for a written decision when an oral statement says no detention or no violation, and do not treat the oral statement as final.

- Report what the police actually recorded and what they said they would do next, without adding a stronger outcome.

## Alternatives for better results

- Ask a trusted person nearby to help with a local police contact when a remote report cannot reach the local station.

- Keep a single dated list of threats already held, instead of reopening old messages to search for more.

- Offer to draft the police statement from the closed evidence set when the user is ready, and stop drafting once it is delivered.
